About me

Designing technology to restore independence and enhance the human experience.

Research & Creative Projects

Capstone Project

Wisk Aero Partnership
Automated Battery Sorting System

As part of my capstone project, I am collaborating with Wisk Aero, a pioneering company in autonomous electric aviation. Our team is developing an innovative Automated Battery Sorting System designed specifically for their factory assembly line to handle battery pouches for their Generation 6 electric VTOL aircraft.

Leadership & Automation

Working as the Team Lead, I tackled a factory automation challenge involving the manual separation of pouch cells based on embedded QR code data.

Technical Systems Integration

I personally focused on integrating a Siemens PLC to create a fully remote, Ethernet-based controlled factory system.

Impact & Innovation

This IoT approach connects the hardware directly through the network, making the robotic cell space vastly more efficient, accessible, and ready to scale for projected demand.

Project Objectives

01 Design an automated system to efficiently sort and organize battery pouches based on quality metrics and specifications
02 Integrate computer vision and sensor systems for real-time quality assessment and defect detection
03 Implement Siemens PLC controllers and remote Ethernet capabilities for overarching system access
04 Ensure compliance with aerospace industry standards and safety protocols

Key Learnings

  • Understanding the critical role of IoT and automation in modern manufacturing
  • Applying robotics engineering principles to solve industry-specific challenges
  • Managing complex project timelines and multidisciplinary teams

Connection to Joy of Living Theme

This research experience directly aligns with my Joy of Living theme by contributing to the advancement of sustainable electric aviation technology. The development of efficient, automated manufacturing systems for electric VTOLs supports the creation of cleaner, more accessible transportation solutions that can enhance quality of life by reducing travel time, decreasing environmental impact, and improving urban mobility.

FSE 150

Introduction to Grand Challenges

Course Experience

FSE 150 served as my gateway into the GCSP, shifting my mindset to understand how engineering solutions have wide-ranging implications on society. We explored the historical context and cultural impacts of emerging technologies.

Analyzing Engineering Methods

Learning from Engineering Failures

Value & Connection to GCSP Theme

I learned that creating technologies to enhance human life isn't just a math problem—it requires understanding historical context, societal impact, and ethical consequences.

Entrepreneurship Competency

Business Viability & Innovation

FSE 301

Entrepreneurship Course

Transforming Devils Prosthetics from Project to Business Venture

The FSE301 entrepreneurship class was a pivotal turning point in my academic journey. It provided the exact framework I needed to take my technical team project, Devils Prosthetics, and transform it into a viable business venture aimed at creating low-cost, 3D-printed myoelectric arms for trans-radial amputees.

Active Listening & Empathy

By engaging with potential prosthetic users, I practiced active listening and empathy to integrate their diverse functional and aesthetic needs into our business models.

Business Model Development

We developed tiered product lines, from the $700 "BudgetBionic" to the $3,000 "BionicsRewired", ensuring our technology could bypass traditional insurance gaps and reach various economic demographics.

Pitch Development

I led the development of our Evidence-Based Pitch Deck, outlining our value proposition, market differentiation, and an actionable IRB approval roadmap for future human testing.

Stakeholder Communication

The pitch skills I gained directly resulted in our team winning additional funding ($10,000 in EPICS Elite Pitch Funding) by successfully communicating our vision to stakeholders.

Connection to Joy of Living Theme

This entrepreneurship experience directly embodies the Joy of Living theme. It taught me that an engineering solution only brings "joy" and independence to people if it is financially accessible and business-viable enough to actually reach the market.

Multicultural Competency

Global Perspectives

ASB 300

Food and Culture

Course Experience

ASB 300 offered a fascinating lens into how food serves as a foundation for understanding diverse social structures, economic values, and cultural traditions.

Participant Observation

For one assignment, I conducted an observation at Infusion Coffee & Tea in Tempe to analyze how the shop acts as a "third place" for community engagement and the exchange of cultural capital.

Cultural Artifacts

By studying food and environments as cultural artifacts, I developed a deeper sensitivity to the varying ways quality of life is defined and nurtured across different contexts.

SLC 212

Languages and Cultures Clash

Course Experience

SLC 212 provided an in-depth examination of how language shapes cultural identity and how miscommunications lead to conflict in diverse settings.

Intercultural Interviews

I applied course concepts by interviewing a physics professor from India to analyze intercultural miscommunication in mainstream American settings.

Navigating Anglo Scripts

We discussed clashes regarding "Anglo scripts," such as navigating indirect feedback, deciphering silence versus hierarchy, and recognizing superficial social invitations.

Value of Multicultural Competency

For my work in robotics and prosthetics, cultural awareness is essential. Different cultures have varying attitudes toward assistive technologies, aesthetic preferences, and unique functional requirements based on their daily traditions. These courses taught me vital strategies for communicating across boundaries—an invaluable skill for collaborating on international engineering teams.
